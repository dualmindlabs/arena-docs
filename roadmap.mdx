## Next <Badge color="blue">Planned</Badge>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Measuring brand bias">
    A controlled study comparing votes when names are shown before voting versus hidden until after. We intend to publish the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prompt difficulty scoring">
    "Say hello" tells you little; a prompt that stresses reasoning tells you a lot. An automatic scorer will estimate how discriminating a prompt is and weight votes accordingly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Multi-turn comparisons">
    Judge models over a whole conversation: context retention, consistent tone, recovering from mistakes, and willingness to update. This needs a new voting interface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Team workspaces">
    Structured evaluation campaigns for teams: shared prompt libraries, private leaderboards, exportable results and roles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Custom rubrics">
    Score accuracy, concision, safety and instruction adherence separately instead of one overall vote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Response diffing">
    See exactly where two answers diverge: wording, structure and length.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Later <Badge color="gray">Exploring</Badge>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Regression testing for model updates" icon="git-compare">
    When a model ships a new version, run the same prompts before and after and show whether it won, lost, or is statistically the same.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Adversarial prompt library" icon="shield-alert">
    A curated set of prompts probing known failure modes (hallucination, sycophancy, refusal calibration, conflicting instructions), run regularly across the roster.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Voice and multimodal battles" icon="image">
    Blind comparisons for image understanding and spoken output. The method carries over; the infrastructure is new.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Research data export" icon="database">
    Anonymised, aggregated vote data in structured formats for academic research.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

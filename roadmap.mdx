# Product Roadmap

We are constantly improving DualMind Arena to provide the most accurate and useful AI model comparisons.

<Info>
Priorities may shift based on community feedback. Have a suggestion? Let us know!
</Info>

## Upcoming Features

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expanded Model Roster">
    Integration of the latest frontier models from major providers as soon as they are released.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Advanced Analytics">
    Deeper insights into model performance:
    - Coding prowess vs. creative writing
    - Instruction following accuracy
    - Verbosity analysis
  </Step>

  <Step title="Team Workspaces">
    Collaborative features for teams to run custom benchmarks and share private comparisons.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Custom Evaluation Rubrics">
    Define your own criteria for "better" — accuracy, speed, conciseness, or safety — for specialized benchmarks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

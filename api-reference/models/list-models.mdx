---
openapi: "GET /api/models"
title: "List models"
---

Use a model's `modelName` (or `modelId`) as `model`, `model1` or `model2`. Cloudflare Workers AI models (`providerName: "cloudflare"`) are only used when you pick them; random picks come from Groq and Google AI Studio. See [Models and providers](/concepts/models-and-providers).

## The Arena

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is DualMind Arena?">
    A free arena at [arena.dualmindlab.tech](https://arena.dualmindlab.tech). In **Battle** mode two anonymous models answer the same prompt; you pick the better one, call a tie, or mark both bad, and the names appear after your vote. **Side by Side** lets you choose the two models, and **Direct Chat** lets you try one on its own. Every vote feeds a live leaderboard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is it free?">
    Yes. Sign in with email, phone, GitHub or Google and start battling.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are the models anonymous?">
    Knowing a model's name changes how you judge its answer. Hiding names until you vote keeps the comparison about the content. See [Evaluation philosophy](/concepts/evaluation-philosophy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which models can I battle?">
    50+ models from Groq, Google AI Studio and Cloudflare Workers AI. Random battles use Groq and Google AI Studio models; Cloudflare models are available when you pick them in Side by Side or Direct Chat. See [Models and providers](/concepts/models-and-providers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An answer starts with “[System Note]”. What happened?">
    The model that was picked was unavailable or took longer than 60 seconds, so a fallback model answered instead. You're comparing the fallback, so consider skipping the vote.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Voting and the leaderboard

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is the leaderboard calculated?">
    With ELO, the rating system used in chess. Every model starts at 1000; winning moves points from the loser to the winner, and beating a stronger model earns more. See [Leaderboard](/concepts/leaderboard).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my vote?">
    No. Each battle takes one vote per person, and it's final. That keeps the leaderboard hard to game.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between “tie” and “both are bad”?">
    **Tie** means both answers were good. **Both are bad** means neither helped. Neither records a winner, but they mean very different things, so please pick the honest one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a model with a high win rate rank lower than one with a lower win rate?">
    ELO weighs **who** a model beat. Many wins against weak opponents count for less than fewer wins against strong ones. Also check the battle count: small samples are noisy.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Threads and privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can see my conversations?">
    Threads are **private** by default: only you can open them. You can make a thread **unlisted** (anyone with the link) or **public** from the share dialog. See [Threads and sharing](/concepts/threads).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I delete a thread?">
    Yes, from the sidebar in the Arena, or with [`DELETE /api/threads/{threadId}`](/api-reference/threads/delete-thread). Deletion can't be undone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where's the privacy policy?">
    At [dualmindlab.tech/privacy](https://dualmindlab.tech/privacy). Privacy questions go to [privacy@dualmindlab.tech](mailto:privacy@dualmindlab.tech).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## API

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a public API?">
    Yes, in beta. It's the same API the Arena uses, at `{{apiBase}}`. Start with the [API overview](/api-reference/introduction).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I authenticate?">
    With the access token from your Arena session, sent as `Authorization: Bearer <token>`. The leaderboard, health check and shared threads need no token. See [Authentication](/api-reference/authentication).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do you have API keys?">
    Not yet. Session tokens are short-lived; long-lived personal API keys are on the [roadmap](/roadmap).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use DualMind for commercial benchmarking?">
    Get in touch at [hello@dualmindlab.tech](mailto:hello@dualmindlab.tech).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
